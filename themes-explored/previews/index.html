Dark Academia — six drafts

Five dark variations on the genre — oxblood, brass, parchment, ink, wood — pitched at distinct axes (most ceremonial → most restrained, warmest → coolest) plus a sixth light counter-proposal that argues the academia is the manuscript page, not the room. Specs in ../DARK_ACADEMIA_THEME_DRAFTS.md. All six render the same Pull view so they sit side-by-side fairly.

DRAFT 01 · da-bodleian
Bodleian Reading Room
Brass on mahogany. The canonical read. Brass desk-lamp pool on the focused card, oxblood wax-seal status dots, silk ribbon hanging from active rows, tooled-leather rim, Latin section heads, Dewey-style IDs in mono.
Brass + Mahogany Lamp pool Wax seals
DRAFT 02 · da-trinity-dusk
Trinity Quadrangle (Dusk)
Tarnished brass on forest-stone. The architectural one — no glow, double-rule chapter heads, square chip nav, gold-leaf primary CTA, small-caps tracked labels. Cooler and more monastic than Bodleian.
Brass + Forest No glow Gold leaf CTA
DRAFT 03 · da-magdalen
Magdalen Candlelit
Beeswax candle on smoke-black. The vibe pick. Single allowed animation — the focused card's lamp pool flickers on a 1200ms sine. Prose time ("quarter past two", "the sixth of May"), serif-only, generous air, inkwell loader.
Beeswax + Smoke Candle flicker Prose time
DRAFT 04 · da-inkwell
Inkwell & Quarto
Iron-black + eggshell + a single drop of oxblood (only on overdue). The Arasaka of the family. No glow, no dots, no ribbon. Active row signaled by an indent shift; marginalia and Dewey IDs do real work. Typography is the academia.
Iron + Oxblood No ornament Marginalia
DRAFT 05 · da-professor
Professor's Study (Autumn)
Burnt sienna on tobacco. The lived-in one. Leather-spine ribbed sidebar, sienna lamp pool, slab-serif section heads, wax-seal dots in sienna, moss-green for done, italic "quarter past two" echoes under deadlines.
Sienna + Tobacco Leather spine Slab heads
DRAFT 06 · da-scriptorium
The Scriptorium (light)
The counter-proposal. A light theme on vellum cream — rubricated oxblood initials on every row title, gold-leaf section dividers, walnut-binding sidebar, Roman-numeral dates, marginalia. Argues the academia is the page, not the room.
Gold leaf + Vellum Rubrication Light theme
