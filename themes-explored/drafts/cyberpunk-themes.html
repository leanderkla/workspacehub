Cyberpunk theme drafts — visual previews

Six previews. Each renders a representative slice of the app — sidebar nav, view header, four todo cards (one focused/active, one done, one with a mention chip and tags, one with overdue), and a button row — using only CSS variables and treatments per draft. Open this file in a browser; widen the window for fairer comparison. Each preview is 540 px tall on a 220-px sidebar, so they stay aligned for side-by-side reading.

1. Bay City Hotel
cp-bay-city
The Raven Hotel after midnight. Heavy curtains, brushed brass fittings, a single amber lamp pooling light onto a marble desk. Built for someone who works at unsociable hours on things that matter.

Round 2 — hybrid + louder drafts

#7 combines the parts you liked from Tachikoma + Yokohama (mono-led density with accent-only-on-focused discipline). #8–#11 push harder into visual chrome: dual accents, status strips, gradient backdrops, faint scanlines, sharp geometry — still within the professional-cyberpunk rails (no glitch effects, no aggressive animations, accents earned not sprayed).

7. Tachikoma Standby
cp-tachikoma-standby
The spider tank in low-power mode — only its eyes glow. Tachikoma's mono-led density and corner cuts; Yokohama's discipline of accent-on-focused-only. Most chrome reads silver-grey on cool slate; lime appears only on the row that has the keyboard cursor.

8. Kowloon Walled City
cp-kowloon
Maze-density operator's terminal. Two accents that mean something — amber for "active right now," violet for "closed / done / archived" — so the eye reads the state-machine before reading the title. Subtle dot grid in the backdrop suggests "this surface is built on data," multi-corner cuts on cards suggest "this surface was machined."

9. Neon Sumida
cp-sumida
Tokyo riverside in late summer. Cyan and magenta locked in tension — cyan reads "active," magenta reads "urgent / human / hot." Strong glows on the focused row, faint horizontal scanlines on the backdrop (1px, low opacity — atmospheric, not distracting). The most "1980s cyberpunk poster" of the set.

11. Razorgirl
cp-razorgirl
Built for moving fast. Warm matte-black base, amber primary, magenta on anything urgent — overdue rows, high-priority todos. Sharp geometric corner markers (top-left + bottom-right brackets on every card; brighter on the focused one) and all-caps tracked headers suggest weaponized software. Less about contemplation, more about operating.
